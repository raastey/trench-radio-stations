Privacy

Trench Radio for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. Trench Radio has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share personal data.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your settings, presets and Wireless Log are stored only on your device. Widgets read them from the app's shared container on the same device. Deleting the app deletes them.

NETWORK REQUESTS

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info) — to find stations and stream addresses. When you tune to a station, the app reports one play to Radio Browser by station ID, as their guidelines ask. No personal data is sent.
  • Broadcasters' stream servers — to play the station you tune to. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address.
  • Internet Archive (archive.org) — to play public-domain old-time radio.
  • GitHub Pages — once a day, to fetch the station list.

CHILDREN

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
