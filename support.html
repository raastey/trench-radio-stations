Trench Radio

A 1917 field wireless that tunes live radio from around the world, and old-time radio from the archive.

TUNING

  • Turn the tuning control. Static and a whistle mean you are between stations; the eye closes and the set locks when you reach one.
  • Press a preset key to tune straight to a station. Long-press a key to store the station you are on.
  • Turn or long-press the band knob for AM, FM, shortwave and the archive.

THE MORSE KEY

Tap R (dot dash dot) to stamp the station in your Wireless Log. Hold the key to open the log. On a Mac press ⌘L.

SETS AND LANGUAGES

Tap the nameplate for Settings. Long-press it to change the set: British, German, French, Russian or Italian. In Settings, or on the band map, choose the broadcast languages that fill the dial.

A STATION WILL NOT PLAY

Live streams sometimes go quiet. The set shows an atmospheric fade, holds the frequency and retries; press DRIFT NOW to move on. In Settings, RE-SURVEY THE ETHER refreshes the station list. Report a broken station at github.com/raastey/trench-radio-stations/issues.
